06 · Apply

Genetics in engineering

🧮 Bioinformatics

Algorithms align and search billions of bases to find genes and mutations.

🔬 DNA sequencing

Nanopore and short-read machines turn chemistry into digital sequence data.

💾 DNA data storage

Encode bits as A, T, G, C – extremely dense, long-lasting storage.

🧬 Genetic algorithms

Selection, crossover and mutation inspire optimisation methods in software.

🤖 AI in genetics

Models predict protein structure and variant effects to speed up discovery.
